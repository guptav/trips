# 6-Day Road Trip: Yellowstone + Grand Teton Return Route

**Route:** Cupertino, CA → Yellowstone National Park → Grand Teton corridor → Cupertino, CA  
**Trip Length:** 6 days total  
**Drive Rule:** Up to 10 hours on Day 1

---

## Trip Overview

| Day | Route | Focus |
|-----|-------|-------|
| 1 | Cupertino → Elko, NV | Long first driving day (up to ~10 hrs) |
| 2 | Elko, NV → Yellowstone (West/South approach) | Reach park and short evening activity |
| 3 | Yellowstone (Full Day) | Geysers + Grand Prismatic area |
| 4 | Start return: Yellowstone → Grand Teton corridor → Idaho side | Scenic Grand Teton pass-through + begin return |
| 5 | Idaho side / Elko region → Northern Nevada or Reno area | Major return leg |
| 6 | Northern Nevada / Reno area → Cupertino | Final drive home |

---

## Day 1 — Cupertino to Elko, Nevada

**Drive:** ~620 miles | up to ~10 hours with breaks  
**Overnight:** Elko, NV

- Depart early from Cupertino via I-580 E → I-80 E.
- Major flexible breaks: Sacramento, Reno, Winnemucca.
- Refuel before late stretches in Nevada.
- Keep this as the longest drive day to protect park time later.

---

## Day 2 — Elko to Yellowstone

**Drive:** ~8–9 hours (entry choice dependent)  
**Overnight:** West Yellowstone, MT or inside park lodging

- Continue east/northeast toward Yellowstone.
- Preferred access for speed: West Entrance (via Idaho).
- If timing allows, do a short evening stop at:
  - Madison area boardwalks, or
  - Old Faithful viewing window.

---

## Day 3 — Yellowstone Full Day (Thermals + Canyon)

**Overnight:** Yellowstone area

Suggested priority loop:
- Old Faithful and Upper Geyser Basin
- Grand Prismatic Spring (boardwalk + overlook if time)
- Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone / Artist Point
- Evening wildlife chance in Hayden Valley pullouts

---

## Day 4 — Start Return Journey via Grand Teton (New Requirement)

**Drive:** Moderate-to-long day (route dependent)  
**Overnight:** Jackson area, Alpine, or Idaho Falls/Pocatello side based on timing

- Begin return from Yellowstone instead of staying another full park day.
- Exit via **South Entrance** and drive through the Grand Teton corridor.
- Sightseeing options during the pass-through (pick 1–3 based on time):
  - Jackson Lake Overlook / Colter Bay viewpoint
  - Oxbow Bend (classic Teton reflections)
  - Mormon Row historic barns
  - Jenny Lake visitor area (short stop)
- After Teton stops, continue south/west to position for faster return home.

> This satisfies your request to start returning on Day 4 while still giving a meaningful Grand Teton viewing window.

---

## Day 5 — Continue Return Toward California

**Drive:** ~8–10 hours (depending on Day 4 endpoint)  
**Overnight:** Elko, Winnemucca, or Reno/Sparks area

- Use I-15/I-86/I-84 connectors as needed, then reconnect to I-80 W.
- Keep stops simple: fuel, meals, short walk breaks.
- Aim to reach western Nevada if possible to shorten Day 6.

---

## Day 6 — Final Leg to Cupertino

**Drive:** ~6–10 hours (based on Day 5 endpoint)

- Follow I-80 W across the Sierra, then into Bay Area corridors.
- Suggested final major stop: Reno/Truckee zone before mountain crossing.
- Arrive Cupertino by evening.

---

## Practical Tips for This 6-Day Version

- Book Yellowstone-area nights first; they are the hardest to secure.
- Carry offline maps (Wyoming/Idaho/Montana/Nevada) due to weak coverage.
- Start early for wildlife and parking at major Yellowstone stops.
- Keep one meal/day flexible to absorb bison traffic delays and construction.
