# 2-Day Road Trip: Cupertino to Solvang via Oceano Dunes

**Route:** Cupertino, CA → Oceano Dunes SVRA → Solvang, CA → Home  
**Travelers:** Adults  
**Total Drive Distance:** ~240 miles one way  
**Best Time to Visit:** Year-round; spring and fall offer the best weather and fewer crowds  
**Departure:** 8:00 AM sharp to maximize the day

---

## Trip Overview

| Day | Route | Highlights |
|-----|-------|------------|
| 1 | Cupertino → Oceano Dunes → Solvang | Coastal drive, dunes, beach, Monarch Butterflies, Solvang evening stroll |
| 2 | Solvang (Full Day) | Mission Santa Inés, wine tasting, museums, Nojoqui Falls hike, Danish village charm |

---

## Packing Essentials

- Sunscreen and sunglasses (intense sun on the dunes and Santa Ynez Valley)
- Sturdy closed-toe shoes (for dune hiking and trail walks)
- Light layers (mornings near the coast can be cool and foggy)
- Reusable water bottles and car snacks for the drive
- Camera / phone fully charged
- Cash for small shops and farm stands in Solvang
- OHV/ATV registration if you plan to bring your own off-road vehicle to the dunes
- Binoculars (optional, great for Monarch Butterflies and bird watching)

---

## Day 1 — Cupertino to Oceano Dunes to Solvang

**Drive (Cupertino → Oceano Dunes):** ~230 miles | ~3.5 hours via US-101 S  
**Drive (Oceano Dunes → Solvang):** ~35 miles | ~45 minutes via CA-1 S and CA-135 N  
**Overnight:** Solvang Downtown

### 8:00 AM – Depart Cupertino

- Hit the road via **US-101 S** toward Gilroy and beyond
- Fill up on gas in Cupertino before heading out — next good fuel stop is Gilroy or King City
- Tune into a road trip playlist and enjoy the transition from Bay Area suburbs to rolling California farmland

### 10:00 AM – Coffee & Breakfast Stop: Paso Robles (~160 miles in)

- Exit US-101 in **Paso Robles** for a well-timed breakfast and stretch (~2 hours from Cupertino)
- **Spoon Trade** or **Thomas Hill Organics** – local favorites with farm-fresh breakfast options
- Paso Robles is also the heart of the Central Coast wine country — you'll pass dozens of winery signs
- Quick stop at **City Park** in downtown Paso Robles for a 10-minute walk to stretch your legs

> 💡 **Tip:** Grab a coffee and use the restroom here — it's the last convenient stop before the dunes.

### 11:45 AM – Oceano Dunes Visitor Center ⭐ (First Stop)

- Continue south on US-101, then take **CA-1 S** toward Oceano
- Arrive at the **Oceano Dunes State Vehicular Recreation Area (SVRA) Visitor Center**
  - Address: 928 Pacific Blvd, Oceano, CA 93445
  - Friendly rangers will brief you on the dunes, current conditions, and access rules
  - Pick up a trail map and learn about the unique dune ecosystem — one of the last coastal dune systems accessible to vehicles in California
  - The center has exhibits on the natural and cultural history of the dunes
  - **Free admission** to the visitor center

### 12:15 PM – Oceano Dunes Exploration 🏖️ ⭐

- **Drive on the Beach** 🚗 – Oceano Dunes is one of the only places in California where you can legally drive a street vehicle on the beach (self-contained, 4WD recommended)
  - Purchase a day-use permit from the kiosk (required for beach/dune access)
  - Drive north along the beach past Oceano to the open dune recreation area
- **Hike the Dunes on Foot** – even without an OHV, you can hike into the tall interior dunes
  - The main dune field reaches heights of 80–100 feet — stunning panoramic views from the crests
  - Walk inland from the beach access ramp toward the dune field; the sand sculpts into wind-carved ridges and bowls
- **Oso Flaco Lake Trail** 🐦 (optional add-on, +20 min drive south on CA-1)
  - A 2.2-mile round-trip boardwalk trail through coastal dunes to a serene freshwater lake
  - Exceptional bird watching: herons, egrets, ducks, and shorebirds
  - Easy, flat, and beautiful — a hidden gem of the Central Coast

> 💡 **Tip:** Wear old shoes or bring sandals — sand gets everywhere. Don't walk barefoot on the upper dunes mid-day; the dark sand heats up quickly.

### 2:30 PM – Pismo Beach Monarch Butterfly Grove 🦋 (15 min north)

- Drive north 10 minutes on CA-1 to the **Pismo Beach Monarch Butterfly Grove**
  - Free to visit; open daily from late October through February (peak season: November–January)
  - Hundreds of thousands of Western monarch butterflies overwinter in the eucalyptus grove here
  - Outside butterfly season, the grove is still a beautiful shaded stop for a walk
  - Docent-led tours available on weekends; the **Friends of the Monarch Butterfly Grove** volunteers are incredibly knowledgeable
- While in Pismo Beach, consider a quick walk to the **Pismo Beach Pier** for ocean views and photos

### 3:30 PM – Late Lunch in Pismo Beach or Grover Beach

- **Steamers of Pismo** – fresh seafood right on the water; clam chowder in a sourdough bowl is legendary
- **Splash Café** – Pismo institution famous for clam chowder and fish tacos (cash/card both accepted)
- Or grab a quick bite at a food truck near the beach access area

### 4:30 PM – Drive to Solvang

- Head east via **CA-1 S → CA-135 N** (aka the Santa Rosa Road/Betteravia Road connector)
- The 35-mile drive winds through gentle agricultural valleys and wine country hills — stunning scenery
- Alternatively, take **US-101 S → CA-246 E** through Buellton for a slightly faster route (~40 min)

### 5:15 PM – Check In: Solvang Downtown Hotel

- **Recommended stays (walkable downtown):**
  - **Hotel Corque** – upscale, modern, pool, right on Mission Drive; rooftop bar views
  - **Landsby Hotel** – Danish-themed boutique hotel in the heart of downtown
  - **Mirabelle Inn** – cozy, historic Danish-style inn; breakfast included
  - **Hadsten House** – courtyard, fireplace rooms, walk to everything
- Drop bags and freshen up before the evening

### 6:00 PM – Solvang Evening Stroll & Dinner 🇩🇰

- Walk **Copenhagen Drive and Mission Drive** — Solvang's main streets lined with half-timbered Danish buildings, windmills, and gas-lit lamp posts
- Stop for an **aebleskiver** (Danish pancake puffs) at **Solvang Restaurant** or **Paula's Pancake House** — a Solvang tradition since 1961
- Explore the charming shops: Dutch bakeries, Scandinavian gift stores, candle shops, and wine boutiques
- **Dinner options:**
  - **Succulent Café** – farm-to-table, locally sourced, excellent cocktails; book ahead
  - **First & Oak** (inside Mirabelle Inn) – upscale wine country cuisine; one of the best restaurants in the valley
  - **Root 246** – creative California cuisine from Chef Bradley Ogden; fantastic wine list
  - **Solvang Restaurant** – classic Danish comfort food (Danish meatballs, æbleskiver, open-faced sandwiches)
- End the evening with a scoop from **Olsen's Danish Village Bakery** (open late) or a nightcap at **Solvang Brewing Company**

> 💡 **Tip:** Book dinner reservations for Day 1 before you leave Cupertino — the best restaurants in Solvang fill up quickly on weekends.

## Day 2 — Solvang Full Day

**Overnight:** Solvang Downtown  
**Theme:** Danish village culture, wine country, history, and nature

### 8:00 AM – Breakfast in Solvang ☕

- **Paula's Pancake House** ⭐ – the quintessential Solvang breakfast; Danish pancakes, crepes, and eggs; expect a short wait on weekends
- **Solvang Bakery** – grab fresh pastries, Danish kringle, and coffee to go
- Enjoy breakfast at an outdoor table watching the town come to life

### 9:00 AM – Mission Santa Inés ⭐ (Must-Do)

- **Old Mission Santa Inés** 🕌 – one of California's 21 historic missions, founded in 1804
  - Address: 1760 Mission Dr, Solvang, CA 93463
  - Beautiful adobe church with hand-painted reredos, original Chumash and Spanish artifacts, and a serene courtyard garden
  - The **mission museum** houses an impressive collection of vestments, bells, and colonial California history
  - The grounds include a rose garden and a restored aqueduct system
  - Small admission fee (~$5); well worth it
  - Open daily from 9:00 AM

### 10:00 AM – Elverhøj Museum of History & Art ⭐

- **Elverhøj Museum** 🏛️ – Solvang's premier cultural institution
  - Address: 1624 Elverhoy Way, Solvang, CA 93463
  - Dedicated to the Danish heritage of Solvang and the history of the Santa Ynez Valley
  - Rotating art exhibits by local and regional artists
  - The museum building itself is a beautifully crafted replica of an 18th-century Danish farmhouse
  - Free admission; donations welcome

### 10:45 AM – Hans Christian Andersen Museum & Downtown Walk

- **Hans Christian Andersen Museum** 📚 – small, charming museum above the Book Loft
  - Celebrates Denmark's most beloved storyteller (The Little Mermaid, The Ugly Duckling)
  - Original prints, manuscripts, and cut-paper art by H.C. Andersen
  - Just a few steps from the main shopping street
- Browse **downtown Solvang's boutique shops:**
  - **Jule Hus** – Christmas ornaments and Danish decorations year-round
  - **Solvang Shoe** – Scandinavian clogs, shoes, and accessories
  - **Village Stationer** – cards, gifts, and local books
  - **Rasmussen's** – fine Scandinavian gifts and collectibles

### 12:00 PM – Wine Tasting in Santa Ynez Valley 🍷 ⭐

- Solvang sits at the heart of the Santa Ynez Valley AVA — world-class Pinot Noir and Chardonnay country (as featured in the film *Sideways*)
- **Downtown tasting rooms (walkable from your hotel):**
  - **Riverbench Vineyard & Winery** – elegant tasting room on Mission Drive; exceptional Chardonnay
  - **Buttonwood Farm & Winery** – a 5-minute drive; charming farm setting, pet-friendly, great Sauvignon Blanc and Merlot
  - **Bridlewood Estate Winery** – scenic hillside estate just outside town; excellent Syrah
  - **Sunstone Vineyards & Winery** – Provençal-style estate; picnic-friendly, stunning gardens
- Tasting fees typically $20–$30 per person; many are waived with a bottle purchase

> 💡 **Tip:** Designate a driver or use Solvang's compact walkable downtown to visit tasting rooms on foot. Several are clustered within a few blocks of the main shopping area.

### 1:30 PM – Lunch in Solvang or at a Winery

- **Solvang Restaurant** – Danish open-faced sandwiches (smørrebrød) and Danish meatball plate
- **Industrial Eats** (in Buellton, 5 min west) – wood-fired pizzas, charcuterie, and local beers; a valley favorite
- **Pea Soup Andersen's** (Buellton) – a Central Coast road trip institution since 1924; try the legendary split pea soup

### 3:00 PM – Nojoqui Falls Park Hike 🌿 ⭐

- **Nojoqui Falls County Park** – a hidden gem just 7 miles south of Solvang
  - Address: Alisal Rd, Solvang, CA 93463 (follow signs from CA-246)
  - Easy 0.5-mile round-trip trail through oak woodland to a 164-foot waterfall
  - The falls flow best in late winter and spring; in summer the flow is lighter but the canyon is still peaceful and shaded
  - Perfect for a post-wine-tasting stroll; the trail is flat and accessible
  - **Free** county park; no reservations needed
  - The picnic area at the trailhead is excellent for a late-afternoon snack break

### 4:15 PM – Ostrich Land USA 🐦 (Optional / Kid-Friendly)

- **Ostrich Land USA** – a quirky and unforgettable stop just west of Solvang on CA-246
  - Address: 610 E CA-246, Buellton, CA 93427
  - Home to over 100 ostriches and emus; you can feed them by hand from a bowl
  - Equal parts hilarious and exciting — ostriches are aggressive and surprisingly fast
  - Small admission fee; open daily; great for all ages

### 5:00 PM – Quicksilver Miniature Horse Ranch (Optional)

- **Quicksilver Miniature Horse Ranch** 🐴 – delightful stop for animal lovers
  - Address: 1555 Alamo Pintado Rd, Solvang, CA 93463
  - World-famous miniature horses; free self-guided tours of the pastures
  - The foals are especially enchanting; horses are friendly and often approach the fence

### 6:00 PM – Golden Hour in Solvang & Farewell Dinner 🌅

- Return to downtown Solvang for golden-hour photos — the windmills and Danish facades glow beautifully at dusk
- Browse any remaining shops before they close (~6–7 PM)
- **Farewell Dinner options:**
  - **First & Oak** – splurge-worthy wine country tasting menu; reserve well in advance
  - **Succulent Café** – lively, creative farm-to-table menu; great cocktails and local wines
  - **Solvang Brewing Company** – craft beers, hearty pub food, outdoor patio; great casual option
  - **Hadsten House Restaurant** – solid Danish-American comfort food; cozy fireplace ambiance

### Evening – Stroll & Wind Down

- After dinner, walk the illuminated downtown streets — the lampposts and windmills create a magical evening atmosphere
- Stop for dessert at **Olsen's Danish Village Bakery** (if open) or pick up local chocolates at **Solvang Sweets**
- Pack up for an early morning departure the next day

> 💡 **Tip:** Sunday traffic on US-101 N from Solvang back to the Bay Area can be heavy after 2 PM. Consider a leisurely 9–10 AM departure to beat the worst of it, or leave earlier.

## Return Drive: Solvang → Cupertino

**Drive:** ~235 miles | ~3.5–4 hours via US-101 N  

- Take **CA-246 W** from Solvang to **US-101 N** via Buellton
- Optional stop: **Paso Robles** for coffee or a winery visit on the way home
- Optional detour: **Hearst Castle** (San Simeon) if you have extra time — take CA-1 N along the coast from San Luis Obispo for spectacular Big Sur-adjacent scenery
