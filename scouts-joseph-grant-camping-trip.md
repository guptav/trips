# Troop 582 Camping Trip: Joseph Grant Park – Big Oak Group Camp

**Location:** Joseph Grant Park, Big Oak Group Camp, San Jose, CA  
**Dates:** Saturday, May 23 – Sunday, May 24, 2025  
**Scouts:** 12  
**Adult Leaders (ASMs):** 3  
**Nights:** 2 camping nights

---

## Saturday, May 23

### 10:00 AM – Departure from Church

Scouts met at the church by 10:00 AM with sack lunches in hand. The troop departed on schedule at 10:30 AM.

### 11:30 AM – Arrival at Joseph Grant Park

The troop arrived at the Big Oak Group Camp at around 11:30 AM. Scouts began setting up tents immediately. The weather was hot, but a large oak tree at the centre of the site provided welcome shade. All tents were pitched by 12:30 PM.

### 12:30 PM – Lunch

Scouts made and ate lunch in the shade from 12:30 PM to 1:15 PM. After the meal, everyone took a moment to relax before the afternoon activities.

### 1:15 PM – Campfire Skit Practice

From 1:15 PM to 2:00 PM, scouts rehearsed their skit for the evening campfire.

### 2:00 PM – TTFC Sessions & Scout's Own

Two back-to-back Tenderfoot / Second Class / First Class (TTFC) sessions ran from 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM:

- **Session 1 (2 hours):** Axes, saws, and jackknives — hands-on instruction and sign-offs. Scouts learned proper technique, safety rules, and used axes to split wood. Exciting and a big hit!
- **Session 2 (2 hours):** First aid — delivered by Miss Leonard, who also shadowed a scout leading portions of the session.

A Scout's Own reflection (approximately 20 minutes) and free time for games and conversation followed the sessions.

> *Thanks to Miss Leonard for leading both TTFC sessions — a total of four hours of dedicated instruction. Her energy made the afternoon both educational and fun.*

### 6:00 PM – Dinner (Dutch Oven)

Dinner was served from 6:00 PM to 7:30 PM. Miss Leonard prepared a delicious **vegetarian lasagna** and a **peach cobbler**, both cooked in a dutch oven. Both dishes were a huge hit with scouts and adults alike.

### 8:00 PM – Joint Campfire with Troop 453/2453

Troop 582 accepted an invitation to join Troop 453/2453 for a campfire at 8:00 PM. Scouts performed skits and played games alongside the other troop. Even an ASM got in on the fun with a skit of their own! **Troop 582 took home the award for best skit.** The evening wrapped up with s'mores.

### 9:30 PM – Astronomy Party

After the campfire, the troop drove to the astronomy party. Highlights of the viewing session included:

- Moon craters in stunning detail
- Jupiter and its four Galilean moons
- Galaxies 27 million light-years away
- Stars and other celestial objects

### 10:15 PM – Lights Out

---

## Sunday, May 24

### 8:00 AM – Wake Up & Breakfast

Scouts woke up at 8:00 AM. Breakfast was finished by 9:30 AM.

### 9:45 AM – Drive to Dutch Flat Trailhead

The troop loaded up and drove to the Dutch Flat Trailhead, arriving around 10:00 AM.

### 10:00 AM – Service Project (Dutch Flat Trailhead)

From 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, scouts completed a **trail maintenance service project**: clearing overgrown branches up to 1 inch in diameter along the Dutch Flat Trailhead. Along the way, scouts learned to identify native plants found on the trail, including **wild cucumber** and other local species.

### 12:15 PM – Return to Camp & Lunch

The troop drove back to the campsite and made and ate lunch from 12:15 PM to 1:45 PM.

### 5:00 PM – Hike: Yerba Buena Trail (~5 Miles)

Scouts set out on the Yerba Buena Trail for approximately a 3-hour hike. The route took them to **Grant Lake**, where the troop hung out for about an hour enjoying the scenery.

### 6:00 PM – Dinner

Dinner was served at the campsite from 6:00 PM to 7:30 PM.

### 8:00 PM – Troop Campfire

The weekend ended with a memorable campfire from 8:00 PM to 9:30 PM, featuring:

- An amazing MC keeping the energy high
- Outstanding skits and songs from the scouts
- A horror-comedy story that kept everyone on the edge of their seats

---

## Summary

The Joseph Grant Park camping trip was a fantastic weekend packed with adventure, learning, and camaraderie. Scouts signed off on **33 requirements**, completed **2 TTFC sessions**, performed a **service project**, hiked **5 miles**, attended an **astronomy party**, enjoyed **2 campfires** (including a joint fire with another troop), and savoured **2 dutch oven meals**. A great trip for Troop 582!
